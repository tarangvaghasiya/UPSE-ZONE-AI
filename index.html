🤖 Doctrine of Basic Structure
A judicial principle that certain fundamental features of the Constitution cannot be altered or destroyed by Parliament.
  • Originates from Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)
  • Prevents damage to the “Spirit of the Constitution”
  • Maintains Supremacy of the Constitution
